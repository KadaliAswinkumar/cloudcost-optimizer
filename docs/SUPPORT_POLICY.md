# CloudCost Optimizer Support Policy

Last updated: May 2026

## 1. Support Channels

- Primary support: `support@cloudcostoptimizer.io`
- Security disclosures: `security@cloudcostoptimizer.io`
- Commercial and contract support: `legal@cloudcostoptimizer.io`

## 2. Priority Levels

- **P1 Critical:** production outage or severe data unavailability.
- **P2 High:** major feature degraded with business impact.
- **P3 Normal:** non-critical defect, guidance, or enhancement request.

## 3. Target Response Times

- **P1:** first response within 1 hour.
- **P2:** first response within 4 business hours.
- **P3:** first response within 1 business day.

## 4. Operating Model

- Triage and acknowledge.
- Confirm impact and scope.
- Provide workaround where available.
- Fix and communicate resolution.
- Share root-cause summary for major incidents.

## 5. Onboarding Support

For new customers, support includes:

- Connector onboarding assistance.
- Initial optimization baseline review.
- Weekly guidance during early implementation phase.

## 6. Escalation

Escalations are available for unresolved P1/P2 issues and are routed to engineering owners.
