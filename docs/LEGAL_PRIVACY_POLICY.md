# CloudCost Optimizer Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

## 1. Scope

This policy describes how CloudCost Optimizer collects, uses, stores, and protects data when customers use the platform.

## 2. Data We Collect

- Account and identity data: name, email, organization details.
- Product usage data: feature usage telemetry, audit trails, lifecycle events.
- Connected cloud/billing metadata: usage, spend, inventory, and optimization context.
- Support and communications: tickets, onboarding notes, and support emails.

## 3. Data Use

We process customer data to:

- Provide FinOps and infrastructure intelligence features.
- Generate recommendations, anomaly signals, and reports.
- Improve product quality, reliability, and support.
- Maintain security and prevent abuse.

## 4. Data Sharing

- We do not sell customer data.
- We may use trusted infrastructure subprocessors strictly for hosting, monitoring, and support operations.
- We may disclose data when required by law.

## 5. Security Controls

- Encryption in transit (TLS) and encryption at rest where applicable.
- Access control and least privilege for internal operations.
- Operational logging and security monitoring.
- Segregation controls for multi-tenant deployments.

## 6. Retention

Customer data is retained only as required for service delivery, legal obligations, and legitimate business continuity purposes.

## 7. Customer Rights

Customers may request:

- Data export.
- Data correction.
- Data deletion (subject to legal retention requirements).

## 8. Contact

For privacy requests: `support@cloudcostoptimizer.io`
